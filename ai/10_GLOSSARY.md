# 4B HUB - Project Glossary

Last Updated: August 2026

---

# Purpose

This document defines the official terminology used throughout the 4B HUB project.

All developers, AI assistants, and documentation should use these terms consistently.

---

# General Terms

## Platform

The complete 4B HUB system.

---

## Public Website

The public-facing company website available to visitors.

---

## Admin CMS

The administration portal used by company staff to manage content and operations.

---

## Employee Portal

The secure portal used by employees for daily work activities.

---

# Employee Terms

## Employee

A company worker with access to the Employee Portal.

---

## Employee Code

The unique identifier assigned to each employee.

Used as the primary login username.

---

## Trusted Device

A registered and approved employee device.

Only trusted devices may perform attendance.

---

## Department

An organizational unit inside the company.

Example:

Operations

HR

Finance

Sales

---

## Job Title

The employee's position.

Example:

Security Guard

Supervisor

Operations Manager

HR Officer

---

# Attendance Terms

## Attendance

The process of recording employee presence.

---

## Check-In

Employee starts the working shift.

---

## Check-Out

Employee ends the working shift.

---

## Shift

The scheduled working period assigned to an employee.

---

## Grace Period

Allowed minutes before an employee becomes late.

---

## Late Minutes

The number of minutes an employee arrives after the grace period.

---

## Overtime

Working time exceeding the scheduled shift.

---

## Absence

Failure to attend a scheduled shift.

---

## GPS Verification

Location validation performed during attendance.

GPS validates attendance but does not block it.

---

## QR Code

A site-specific code scanned during attendance.

---

## Checkpoint

A physical location identified by a QR Code.

Future versions will support multiple checkpoints per site.

---

## Guard Tour

A patrol consisting of multiple checkpoint scans.

---

# Leave Terms

## Leave Request

A request submitted by an employee for time off.

---

## Leave Balance

Remaining leave days available to the employee.

---

## Leave Status

Pending

Approved

Rejected

Cancelled

---

# Payroll Terms

## Basic Salary

Fixed monthly salary.

---

## Bonus

Additional payment.

---

## Penalty

Deduction caused by policy violations.

---

## Deduction

Any reduction from the employee's salary.

---

## Net Salary

Final salary after calculations.

---

# Website Terms

## Dynamic Content

Content loaded from the CMS and database.

---

## Static Website

Legacy HTML generated by build.py.

Scheduled for removal.

---

# Technical Terms

## Repository

Responsible only for database access.

---

## Service

Responsible for business logic.

---

## Prisma

Official ORM used by the project.

---

## RBAC

Role-Based Access Control.

---

## CMS

Content Management System.

---

## API

Route handlers exposing application services.

---

# Future Terms

Additional terminology should always be documented here before being used across the project.
